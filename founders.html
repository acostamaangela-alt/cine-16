The two behind it.

Two ways of seeing, one production company.

Founder / Cinematographer

Ali Al Shehabi

Ali is a Bahraini visual artist working as a photographer and cinematographer. Born in Bahrain, raised in the UAE, trained in Tokyo. He shoots stills and serves as director of photography on commercial and editorial productions.

His images have appeared in The New Yorker, Time Magazine, GQ Middle East, the Qatar Biennale, and the Sharjah Art Foundation. Grantee of the Arab Documentary Photography Program with Magnum Photos, AFAC, and the Prince Claus Fund.

Founder / Creative Director

Angela Acosta

Angela works across photography, illustration, writing, and creative direction. The four sit on the same desk and the work moves between them. Born in the Philippines, raised by the world, now based in Bahrain.

Her practice runs on observation: memory, place, the passing of time. The same attention shows up in commercial work, where she handles concept, copy, and art direction.
