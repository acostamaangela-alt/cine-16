003 / About

The studio, the name.

A production company built on the basics of photography, done well.

01

Origin

Cine 16 takes its name from the sunny 16 rule. When the sun is out, set your aperture to f/16 and trust the light. Old photography lore, from before light meters and autofocus.

We named the studio after it because the rule says something we believe. That instinct, trained into practice, is the foundation of good work. We bring it to every project we take on, at any scale.

02

Approach

We treat every brief as a chance to look properly. To read the light, the space, the subject, and respond to what's there. Less stylistic signature, more attention paid.

The work spans commercial campaigns, editorial, hospitality, and personal commissions. The thread between them is the same: considered framing, technical control, and the patience to wait for the picture that earns its place.

03

Practice

Photography. Stills for brands, publications, and personal commissions.

Videography. Commercials, brand films, and longer-form work. Concept to final cut.

Also offered: social media direction and illustration commissions on request.

04

Working with us

We work with brands, publications, hotels, and people who care about the work being made carefully. Based in Manama. Available wherever the assignment takes us.
